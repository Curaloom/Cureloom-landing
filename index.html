The Curalooms method

AI extracts.
Rules decide.

01Extract

Start with the files you already have.

Upload an EHR export, care plan, or roster. Curalooms finds the facts without asking your team to change systems.

02Decide

Principles

How we
build it.

01

File-first

No integration, no IT project, no new system of record. Start with the data you have.

02

Deterministic

Clear rules applied the same way every time. No black box deciding what gets billed.

03

Auditable

A source pointer, confidence score, and model version behind every extracted fact.

04

Human-led

AI only extracts. The rules decide. A human signs off before a claim leaves the practice.

Patient data

How we handle
patient data.

Start without PHI

Your first month can run on a de-identified export. You see the results before any patient-identifiable data, or a BAA, is involved.

BAA first

We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any identifiable patient data is shared, and answer your security questionnaire before that.

No EHR access

File-first means we never connect to your EHR or hold credentials to it. You choose exactly what you upload.

Every action logged

Who uploaded what, which rule fired, who signed off — every step is recorded and can be walked back to its source.
